shingeki no kyojin

Shingeki no Kyojin, also known as Attack on Titan, is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hajime Isayama. The story is set in a world where humanity resides within enormous walled cities to protect themselves from the Titans, gigantic humanoid creatures that devour humans.

As the walls begin to fall, Eren Yeager and his friends uncover the secrets behind the Titans and fight for humanity's freedom. Their journey explores courage, sacrifice, loyalty, and the true cost of freedom.
